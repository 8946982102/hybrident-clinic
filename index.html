FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is dental treatment painful?

At HybriDent, we use advanced painless techniques and local anesthesia to ensure you feel no discomfort during any procedure.

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend a checkup and cleaning every 6 months. However, our doctors might suggest more frequent visits based on your dental condition.

Do you offer emergency dental services?

Yes, we provide 24/7 emergency dental care. Call us immediately for severe toothaches, broken teeth, or other dental emergencies.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we accept most major dental insurance plans. Contact our front desk to verify your coverage and benefits.
